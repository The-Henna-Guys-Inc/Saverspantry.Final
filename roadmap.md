# App Store submission checklist

- [x] Step 1 (app-side): Replace the generic iOS icon with Saver's Pantry branding.
- [ ] Step 1 (on Mac): Pull latest changes, build and sync iOS, archive a new build, upload to TestFlight, and confirm its icon.
- [ ] Step 2: Test the uploaded build on iPhone via TestFlight.
- [ ] Step 3: Complete App Store listing details (description, keywords, category, age rating).
- [ ] Step 4: Upload the required iPhone and iPad screenshots.
- [ ] Step 5: Confirm Privacy Policy and Terms URLs.
- [ ] Step 6: Enter App Review login and testing notes.
- [ ] Step 7: Complete App Privacy questionnaire.
- [ ] Step 8: Complete content rights declaration.
- [ ] Step 9: Choose build and pricing/availability, then submit for review.
